# RSCIT – Rajasthan State Certificate in Information Technology

## Course Overview • कोर्स विवरण

**RSCIT (Rajasthan State Certificate in Information Technology)** is the premier IT literacy certification recognized across Rajasthan. Approved by the Government of Rajasthan and conducted under the aegis of RKCL & VMOU Kota, this course is designed to empower students, job seekers, and homemakers with modern computer and digital skills.

At **Bhavya Career Institute**, we do not merely prepare students to clear the exam—we ensure that every candidate masters actual computer usage with 100% practical lab practice.

### Why Choose Bhavya Career Institute for RSCIT?

- **Authorized & Recognized Training Environment**: Modern high-spec computer lab in Kota with individual 1:1 PC allocation for each student.
- **Experienced Faculty**: 15+ years experienced trainers guiding each module in both Hindi and English.
- **Complete Exam Support**: Complete mock test series, online assessments portal support, and doubt clearing sessions.
- **Career Readiness**: Essential typing practice (Hindi & English) alongside MS Office skills so you can immediately work in government or corporate offices.

### Career Opportunities After RSCIT

1. **State Government Jobs**: Required for Patwari, VDO, LDC, High Court Clerk, and Police recruitment.
2. **Office Executive / Data Entry Operator**: Employment in private firms, schools, hospitals, and legal offices.
3. **Emitra / CSC Center Operator**: Self-employment running government service kiosks and cyber cafes.
