# DCA – Diploma in Computer Applications

## Diploma Overview • डिप्लोमा का परिचय

The **Diploma in Computer Applications (DCA)** is an intensive, career-oriented certification designed for students seeking an all-round qualification in computing. From foundational typing and office productivity to database management, GST accounting, and web layout design, this program builds complete professional readiness.

Whether you aim to work as an Office Administrator, Accountant, Data Analyst, or Customer Support Lead, DCA equips you with the complete toolkit required in modern workplaces.

### Key Highlights of DCA at Bhavya Career Institute

- **Full Practical Lab Exposure**: Daily scheduled computer access with dedicated instructors answering queries in real-time.
- **Multi-Disciplinary Syllabus**: Learn both administrative skills (MS Office, Access) and financial accounting (Tally Prime + GST).
- **Government Recognized Certification**: Valid nationwide for clerical and computer operator recruitment.
- **Free Career Guidance**: Regular resume critiques, interview mock sessions, and access to campus interview drives.
