# Generative AI & Prompt Engineering Masterclass

## Transform into an Industry-Ready AI Practitioner

Generative AI is transforming modern work across software engineering, business automation, and content creation. This comprehensive course takes you from foundational prompting concepts to building production-ready AI applications, autonomous agents, and RAG-powered knowledge bases.

### Capstone Projects Included:

- **Enterprise Document Assistant (RAG)**: Chatbot that answers questions accurately based on company PDFs, manuals, and policies using vector embeddings.
- **Autonomous Customer Support Agent**: Multi-turn AI agent that looks up order statuses in a database, triggers email notifications, and routes complex queries.
- **Automated Content & Research Studio**: Pipeline that analyzes industry news, generates structured research briefs, and drafts social media campaigns automatically.
