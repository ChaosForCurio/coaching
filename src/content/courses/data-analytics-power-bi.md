# Data Analytics & Business Intelligence with Power BI & Python

## Turn Raw Data into Actionable Business Insights

Organizations run on data. The demand for skilled analysts who can connect databases, uncover patterns, and build executive dashboards is at an all-time high. This course provides comprehensive, hands-on training to prepare you for high-growth data roles.

### Capstone Projects Included:

- **E-Commerce Executive Sales Dashboard**: Track sales revenue, profit margins, product return rates, and customer lifetime value using Power BI and DAX.
- **HR & Employee Attrition Analytics**: Uncover patterns causing employee turnover, evaluate performance scores, and forecast hiring needs.
- **Financial Performance & Cash Flow Tracker**: Automated monthly financial statement analysis with Power Query and SQL.
