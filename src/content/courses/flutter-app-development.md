# Mobile App Development with Flutter & Dart

## Build Cross-Platform Mobile Apps for Android & iOS

Flutter by Google has become the industry standard for rapid, high-performance cross-platform mobile development. This course equips you with complete mastery of Flutter, Dart, state management, and cloud backends.

### Capstone Projects Included:

- **Food Delivery & Tracking App**: Real-time order placement, geolocation tracking with Google Maps, and payment integration.
- **Social Chat & Media App**: One-on-one real-time messaging, image sharing, online presence, and Firebase push notifications.
- **Institute Student Companion App**: Attendance tracking, lecture notes download, batch notifications, and offline reading support.
