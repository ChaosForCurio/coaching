# Top 10 Computer Skills Employers Want in Kota 2025

We spoke with businesses, CAs, coaching institutes, and retail shops across Kota. We analysed hundreds of local job listings. Here is what employers are actually looking for in 2025.

---

## 1. Tally Prime (Accounting Software)

**Demand Level: 🔥 Very High**

Nearly every business in Kota that deals with inventory, sales, or taxes needs someone who knows Tally. With GST becoming more complex, Tally Prime proficiency is the #1 skill employers mention.

**Who needs it:** Shops, factories, CAs, coaching institutes, hospitals

---

## 2. MS Excel (Intermediate to Advanced)

**Demand Level: 🔥 Very High**

Basic Excel is common. But employers want people who know:
- VLOOKUP / HLOOKUP
- IF, SUMIF, COUNTIF formulas
- Pivot tables and charts
- Data validation and filters

These skills save businesses hours of manual work.

---

## 3. Data Entry (Speed + Accuracy)

**Demand Level: 🔥 High**

Every organization generates data. Accurate, fast data entry (30+ WPM) with basic MS Excel knowledge is required for thousands of administrative and back-office jobs.

---

## 4. MS Word (Document Processing)

**Demand Level: 📊 High**

Letters, proposals, notices, and reports — all require MS Word. Mail merge for bulk communication, proper formatting, and PDF creation are key skills.

---

## 5. Basic Internet & Email (Gmail, Google Drive)

**Demand Level: 📊 High**

Many employers are surprised that candidates can not send a professional email or share a file on Google Drive. These basic digital communication skills are now a minimum requirement.

---

## 6. Digital Marketing (Social Media & Google)

**Demand Level: 📈 Growing**

As businesses in Kota shift to online marketing, they need staff who understand:
- Facebook and Instagram advertising
- Google My Business management
- WhatsApp for Business
- Basic SEO concepts

Even local shops are hiring part-time digital marketing assistants.

---

## 7. Python (for Data & Automation)

**Demand Level: 📈 Growing**

IT companies and coaching institutes in Kota are hiring Python-literate candidates for automation, data analysis, and scripting. Entry-level Python knowledge can open remote work opportunities worth ₹20,000+/month.

---

## 8. RSCIT / Computer Literacy Certificate

**Demand Level: 🏛️ Essential for Government Jobs**

For any government job application in Rajasthan, RSCIT is now a baseline requirement. Even private employers prefer candidates with certified computer training.

---

## 9. Typing (Hindi + English)

**Demand Level: 📊 Steady**

30 WPM in English and/or 20 WPM in Hindi Mangal font is a standard requirement for government computer operator posts. Many corporate jobs also need fast typists.

---

## 10. MS PowerPoint (Presentation Skills)

**Demand Level: 📊 Moderate**

Schools, coaching institutes, and corporate teams regularly need PowerPoint presentations. Basic to intermediate skills — slide design, animations, charts — are valued.
