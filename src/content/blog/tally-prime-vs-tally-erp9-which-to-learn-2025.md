# Tally Prime vs Tally ERP 9 — Which Should You Learn in 2025?

If you are planning to learn Tally, you have probably heard about both **Tally Prime** and **Tally ERP 9**. Which one should you learn? The answer is clear in 2025 — but let us break it down.

---

## What is Tally ERP 9?

Tally ERP 9 was the industry standard accounting software for over a decade. Released in 2009, it became the go-to tool for:
- Business accounting
- Inventory management
- GST filing (after 2017)
- Payroll processing

However, **Tally ERP 9 reached end-of-life in 2021** and is no longer actively updated by Tally Solutions.

---

## What is Tally Prime?

**Tally Prime** (launched in November 2020) is the successor to Tally ERP 9. It includes all the features of ERP 9, plus:

- Completely redesigned, modern interface
- Simplified GST filing and reports
- Multi-tasking — open multiple companies at once
- Faster navigation and search
- Go To bar — find any feature quickly
- Better exception reports
- Connected services (online data sync)

---

## Key Differences

| Feature | Tally ERP 9 | Tally Prime |
|---------|-------------|-------------|
| Interface | Older, menu-heavy | Modern, intuitive |
| GST Filing | Manual, complex | Simplified, guided |
| Multi-company | Limited | Full multi-tasking |
| Updates | Discontinued | Actively updated |
| In Demand (2025) | Declining | ✅ High demand |
| Learning Curve | Steeper | Easier for beginners |

---

## Which Do Employers in Kota Want?

We surveyed local businesses, CAs, and shops in Kota. The verdict for 2025:

- **95% of new job listings** mention Tally Prime specifically
- Most accounting firms have already migrated from ERP 9 to Prime
- Government offices using Tally have also switched to Prime
- GST compliance is much easier with Tally Prime

**Conclusion: Learn Tally Prime. Tally ERP 9 knowledge is a bonus, but Prime is what employers want.**

---

## Should You Learn Both?

If you already know Tally ERP 9, upgrading to Tally Prime takes just **1–2 weeks** of practice since the core concepts are identical. The interface and navigation are different, but the underlying accounting logic is the same.

For **new learners**, start directly with Tally Prime. There is no reason to learn ERP 9 from scratch.
