# RSCIT Exam Complete Guide for Rajasthan Students 2025

The **Rajasthan State Certificate in Information Technology (RSCIT)** is one of the most important computer certifications for students and job seekers in Rajasthan. This guide covers everything you need to know to clear the RSCIT exam in 2025.

---

## What is RSCIT?

RSCIT is a government-authorised IT literacy certificate issued by **RKCL (Rajasthan Knowledge Corporation Limited)**. It is recognised by the Rajasthan government and is **mandatory for most government job applications** in the state.

---

## Why is RSCIT Important?

- Required for 65%+ of Rajasthan government posts
- Mandatory for police constable, clerk, patwari, and teacher recruitment
- Accepted for private sector jobs as proof of basic IT literacy
- Course duration is only 3 months — quick and affordable

---

## RSCIT Syllabus 2025

The RSCIT course covers:

1. **Computer Fundamentals** — hardware, software, input/output devices
2. **Windows Operating System** — files, folders, shortcuts
3. **MS Word** — documents, formatting, tables
4. **MS Excel** — spreadsheets, formulas, charts
5. **MS PowerPoint** — presentations, animations
6. **Internet & Email** — browsing, Gmail, social media safety
7. **Government Portals** — Jan Aadhaar, DigiLocker, Rajasthan e-mitra, Paytm

---

## RSCIT Exam Pattern

| Section | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Mode | Online (computer-based) |
| Questions | 35 MCQs |
| Marks | 70 (2 marks each) |
| Passing Marks | 28 (40%) |
| Duration | 1 hour |
| Practical | Yes (separate assessment) |

---

## RSCIT Exam Fees

- Course fee: approximately ₹3,000–₹3,500 (including exam fee)
- Paid to the authorised RKCL center directly

---

## RSCIT Preparation Tips

1. **Practice on computer daily** — the exam is online, so familiarity with the keyboard and mouse matters
2. **Focus on MS Office** — Excel and Word questions make up a large portion
3. **Learn government portals** — Jan Aadhaar, DigiLocker, and e-mitra are frequently tested
4. **Attempt mock tests** — solve previous year question papers
5. **Join a certified center** — coaching from experienced faculty makes a big difference
